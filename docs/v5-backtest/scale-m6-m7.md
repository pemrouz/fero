## Scale numbers

- Design envelope: members 1-100 (n=100 tier), replicas 1-5 (design point R=3), W = majority of the R-set; 3,000 partitions measured, sized for 1,000,000 (~30,000 held per node at n=100 R=3); ~1,000 writes/s cluster-wide. (DESIGN-SCALE §1)
- Relay cost ~110 µs per relayed record (2026-10-09); no relay perf pin exists. (DESIGN-SCALE §1, §8)
