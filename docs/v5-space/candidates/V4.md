# V4: fero v4 as built, with leaderless last-writer-wins replication per cell

**Verdict.** On VERDICT's frame (strict DATA-01, clauses restated, no loss accepted; see the [key of terms][key]), v4 is out on 50 MUSTs, the most of any row. Most come from one choice: any member takes a write, and each cell keeps the write with the higher Lamport stamp. So no single writer judges a condition: two sells of the last item through two servers both answer ok. Its own simulator also pins F-01 and F-02 red. The loose DATA-01 reading changes nothing, since v4 fails DATA-01 by structure on every reading. Accepting JetStream's default test line as a loss changes nothing either: v4 manages about 250 `persisted` writes a second per member. As worded it fails the same 50 and ranks 20th of 20 ([A.2][oa2]).

## In short

- **Where writes go.** Any member takes a write. A hashed ring names an owner for each partition, and writes are forwarded there.
- **How replicas agree.** They do not vote on writes. Each cell keeps the write with the higher Lamport stamp, and members compare digests to heal. Membership changes by joint-consensus ballot.
- **Who heals and moves.** Each member heals itself. There is no controller and no slot move.
- **What a commit means.** A ladder chosen per write (Q `mix`). The disk is synced once per write step, with no group commit.
- **Where rows fold.** Every member folds the rows with `data`. Kernel, fold and user code share one event loop.
- **Where it comes from.** SPACE's V4 row, "v4 as it is" ([SPACE 4.2][s42]). Its replication follows Dynamo, Cassandra and Riak ([SPACE 2][s2]). It takes no fix ([AMENDMENTS 3][am3]).

## What fails, and the proof

One line per MUST, from the [scorecard][card]. "v4's sim" means v4's own simulator, where the case is pinned red today (STATUS.md). No restatement and no item of [section 8][s8] saves any of them. SC-03, SC-10 and P-05 fail restated too.
- **P-01 Live shared state.** Structural: two members apply writes to one row in opposite orders.
- **P-02 Cache with expiry.** Structural: no time rule and no log position per partition.
- **P-05 WebSocket fan-out.** Ceiling: about 9,000 deliveries a second against P-05's 100,000 ([E-08]). Gap 0.91.
- **P-06 Pub/sub.** Structural: two publishes through two members reach subscribers in different orders.
- **P-07 Streams and replay.** Structural: a partition has no single sequence of records.
- **P-08 Consumer groups.** Structural: two members can each take one partition, and a zombie's commit is not refused.
- **P-09 Work queue.** Structural: two workers can take one message through two members, and both are told ok.
- **P-10 Locks.** Structural: two members can each grant the lock.
- **P-11 Counters.** Structural: two increments that each fit can cross the bound, and last-writer-wins can lose one.
- **P-13 Presence.** Structural: no time rule and no single writer to judge a lapsed session.
- **P-14 Invariants.** Structural: two sells of the last item through two servers both pass, as v4 measured.
- **P-15 Transactions across partitions.** Structural: no unit across partitions, and no fenced locks to build one ([E-24]).
- **P-16 Event sourcing.** Structural: compaction drops every entry that is not its cell's winner.
- **P-17 Derived resources.** Counterexample: a window applies a derived output twice after a restart past its horizon ([E-30], 12 to 13 steps; [E-44]).
- **P-20 Change the shape of rows.** Structural: no full history and no shape version.
- **P-22 Delayed jobs.** Structural: each member judges a take by its own clock.
- **PERF-02 Redis 7.** Structural: about 250 `persisted` writes a second per member against Redis's 88,600 to 91,600 SETs. Gap 0.997.
- **PERF-03 NATS core.** Structural: about 9,000 relayed records a second per core against NATS's 2.25 million. Gap 0.996.
- **PERF-04 NATS JetStream.** Structural: 250 a second against JetStream's 1,228 acks a second with fsync always ([E-09b]). Gap 0.93.
- **PERF-05 Kafka.** Structural: 250 a second against 86,344 records a second. Gap 0.997.
- **PERF-06 Redpanda.** Structural: 250 a second against 175,420 records a second. Gap 0.999.
- **PERF-07 etcd.** Structural: 250 a second against etcd's 1,118 puts at one client; no conditional write. Gap 0.99.
- **PERF-10 Reads and ops.** Structural: no conditional op at a writer and no linearizable read.
- **PERF-11 Fan-out.** Structural: about 9,000 deliveries a second per relay core against 100,000. Gap 0.91.
- **PERF-13 Nothing on a timer grows with data.** Structural: compaction rescans every large lane each heartbeat (43 % of CPU in one run).
- **DATA-01 data is the state engine.** Structural: a last-writer-wins compare, with no log order, decides each cell.
- **DATA-07 Rows handed out are immutable.** Structural: handed-out rows are the stored values (an open v4 bug).
- **SC-03 Scale writes up.** Ceiling: the ring places writers with no move that follows load ([E-18]). Gap 0.66.
- **SC-05 Scale to zero and back.** Structural: state lives only in each member's dir, with no tier.
- **SC-08 Without worry.** Model: v4's sim commits one attempt twice when a slow member joins.
- **SC-10 Only stated ceilings.** Ceiling: the same load-blind placement ([E-18]). Gap 0.66.
- **SC-13 Many resources.** Structural: each resource is a cluster with its own port and heartbeats.
- **F-01 No lost acknowledged write.** Structural: v4's sim ("short lane (durable)").
- **F-02 Exactly once per attempt.** Structural: v4's sim commits a stranded write twice after a retry.
- **F-03 One writer.** Structural: v4's genesis is open multi-writer last-writer-wins.
- **F-04 Nothing seen is undone.** Structural: v4's sim ("zombie lane").
- **F-07 Restart in any order.** Structural: only the last member to stop can bring the cluster back.
- **F-08 Deleted rows stay deleted.** Structural: v4's sim brings a removed row back after compaction.
- **F-09 Every loss is said.** Structural: a member that turns read-only prints nothing.
- **F-14 User code never stops fero.** Structural: a main-thread kernel went silent 2.6 to 5.1 s in a 5 s stall ([E-07]).
- **F-21 Divergence is caught.** Structural: members compare lane digests only, never the state hash.
- **DEP-02 Caught up is a number.** Structural: v4 reports no caught-up number.
- **DEP-05 Seeds once.** Structural: a deleted seed row can come back once its tombstone is collected.
- **DEP-12 Group commit.** Structural: one fsync per write. Gap 0.995.
- **DEP-18 Projects never meet.** Structural: v4 has no project, so two apps with one name meet.
- **TEST-13 Outside checkers.** Structural: two ok sells of one item have no linear order.
- **OPS-01 No numbers to choose.** Structural: growth needs `replicas: N`, a number the user picks.
- **OPS-03 Config.** Structural: two servers whose cluster values differ refuse to link.
- **OPS-05 Mixed versions.** Structural: no cluster version; mixed versions are unsupported (V4-14).
- **OPS-06 Security.** Structural: one secret, with no rotation window.

## What it does well

- **It exists and it runs.** So 12 cells are met here and open for all three leading rows (COMP2, MIN-P+par and TiKV-TS; computed here: `repro diff V4 COMP2 MIN-P+par TiKV-TS`). They are DATA-03, DATA-06, SC-01, SC-07, F-15, DEP-01, DEP-03, DEP-04, DEP-13, TEST-10 and OPS-14 (MUSTs), and DEP-19 (S).
- **Some of them, measured on v4 itself.** Four rolls of three durable members took 3.6 to 5.0 s each against 10 s, with no failed call (DEP-01). A restart of all was writable again in 0.7 to 1.9 s against 2 s (DEP-03). Its simulator runs the same kernel bytes as its shell (TEST-10).
- **The fewest open MUSTs of any row: 64** ([VERDICT 6][v6]). More of its cells are decided, because it exists and was run.
- **Few pairs: 52.** Only BUY (12) has fewer ([VERDICT 7.6][v76]).
- **It writes on both sides of a split** ([SPACE 4.2][s42]). Several v5 bars are v4's own figures, such as DEP-13's.

## What would change its verdict

- **Nothing.** No reading, loss or restatement saves any of its 50 failures. V4 is the baseline that v5 must beat.
- **Item 20 would lower it further.** With DEV-01's bar read the other way, v4's own DEV-01 fails too. So does PERF-08, with its phase read the other way (computed here: `repro tally V4 --worded --choice other`).
- **No kill criterion of [section 9][s9] bears on it.** No experiment is owed to it. The all-row fixes A-05, A-07, A-09, A-10 and A-13 would apply only if v5 built on it ([AMENDMENTS 3][am3]).

## Twins and neighbours

## The evidence

- [E-07][]: a main-thread kernel went silent 2.6 to 5.1 s during a 5 s stall; on a worker it kept its heartbeat within 5 ms.
- [E-30][]: a dedup window applies a derived output twice after a restart past its horizon (12 to 13 steps).
- [E-12][]: a log per group loses to one shared journal from 256 groups (p99 4.5 to 47 times worse).
- [E-34][]: v4's own codec woke 10 GB of rows in 86 to 122 s in one member.
- [E-42][]: v4, like BUY, cannot get the always-on recorder of kernel inputs.
- [E-41][]: $0.19 asleep and $7.02 a month at 10 writes a second (E-41's first prices; the re-check did not price V4). It cannot run 5,000 writes a second.
