# E-17 part 1: results

Generated by `harness/report.py` from `results/<option>-ablation.json`. An option name with `+giveup` is the same model with its give-up path pinned (never dropped). `LIMIT` means the run stopped at 1.5 million states with no counterexample: undecided. An undecided rule is kept and marked `undecided`; an undecided mutant is never counted as caught. Kept = pinned + undecided + necessary (removing it breaks a property). Properties: **P1** no acknowledged write lost or extra: every group that holds a partition open has exactly its history as rows; **P2** a position never changes meaning: no position names two writes, and positions rise along each partition's history; **P3** dedup and fence state survive: no attempt applied twice, lock tokens never fall along a partition's history, and an attempt told "refused" never commits; **P4** a cursor resumes with no gap or overlap: what the reader took is always a prefix of the history, and only ever grows; **P6** one writer per partition: at most one group holds it open; **L4** the reader can always catch up (no cursor left stuck); **L5** every move or re-key under way can end once faults and timers stop; **L5b** grow and shrink finish: in every state reached without a give-up timer (cancel, abort, abandon), every planned change can still complete; **L7** every attempt can get an answer (applied or refused) once faults and timers stop; **L5g** a give-up gives the partition back: once a move or re-key is given up (cancel, abort, abandon committed), calm steps can reach a state where its source, and only its source, holds the partition open.
