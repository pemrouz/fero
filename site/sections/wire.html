On the wire

A write crosses the wire as one frame: a short header, the writer, the counter it starts at, the view number, then the records. Most also carry the attempt: who issued the write, and its own counter for it. Below is a real frame from the cluster above, taken apart by kernel/codec.ts. The owner writes these same bytes to its write-ahead log (the WAL, its log on disk), and the page checks them byte for byte. When a node comes back, each side sends a digest, the highest counter it holds from each writer, and the other sends only what is missing.
